# Sea Breeze Homestay, Da Nang

Host: Linh (speaks Vietnamese and English). Host phone for emergencies only: +84 900 000 000

## Check-in and check-out
- Check-in from 14:00. Check-out by 11:00.
- The lockbox is on the left of the green gate. The host sends the code on the morning of arrival.
- Early check-in or late check-out: ask the host, it depends on other bookings.

## The house
- 2 bedrooms, 1 bathroom, kitchen with fridge, kettle and rice cooker.
- Air conditioning: the remote is in the drawer under the TV. Press the snowflake button for cooling.
- Hot water: switch on the red heater switch outside the bathroom 10 minutes before a shower.
- Washing machine on the rooftop. Detergent is in the cupboard next to it.

## House rules
- No smoking inside. Quiet after 22:00. No parties.
- Pets are not allowed.

## Around the house
- My Khe Beach: 5 minutes on foot, walk east to the end of the lane.
- Breakfast: Bánh mì Bà Lan at the corner of the lane, opens 6:00.
- Seafood dinner: Bé Mặn on Võ Nguyên Giáp street.
- Grab works well for taxis and food delivery.
